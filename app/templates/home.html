Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our SaaS platform.

What is a Multi-Tenant SaaS Platform?

A multi-tenant platform allows multiple companies to use the same application while keeping their data secure and isolated.

Can I manage multiple branches?

Yes. You can manage multiple branches, departments, employees, and customers from one dashboard.

Does the platform support billing and invoices?

Yes. The system provides invoice generation, payment tracking, GST billing, and receipt management.

Is my company data secure?

Yes. Each tenant's data is completely separated and protected using secure authentication and database isolation techniques.
